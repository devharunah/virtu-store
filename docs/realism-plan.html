Track A · your avatar (free: Avaturn + Mixamo)

Meshy's API needs the Pro plan, so we use two free tools instead. You do the web steps (they need your sign-in); I script everything after the downloads.

  1. Avaturn (avaturn.me, free account): create an avatar from 3 selfies (front, left, right). Pick a white shirt + dark trousers, then Export → GLB. Save it as blender/source/avatar/avaturn.glb.
  2. Me: I convert it into a Mixamo-ready file, blender/source/avatar/for-mixamo.fbx.
  3. Mixamo (mixamo.com, free Adobe ID): Upload Character → that FBX → confirm the auto-rig markers.
  4. In Mixamo, download 4 FBX files at 30 fps into blender/source/avatar/: Idle (With Skin) → idle.fbx; Walking, Running (Without Skin, tick In Place) → walk.fbx, run.fbx; Jump (Without Skin) → jump.fbx.
  5. Me: blender/avatar.py merges the 4 clips, sets your height, shrinks the textures, and replaces the walking character. You then see yourself in the shop.

Selfie checklist (Avaturn)

  • Front, then turn your head left and right (Avaturn guides you)
  • Even daylight on your face, no hat or glasses, neutral expression
  • Plain background behind you
